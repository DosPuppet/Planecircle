# Accuracy of mythic, rare and uncommon cards — 2026-10-03

> **Status on 2026-10-10 (PLAN-M M0):** the deferred list (Prismabasher, Likeness Looter, Moseo, Head of the Hunt, Superior Spider-Man, Faerie Fencing, Social Snub, Hylda) and the EOE "noted, not fixed" cards (Faller's Faithful, Sunstar Expansionist, Singularity Rupture) are done; Moonlit Meditation, Raiding Schemes, Sidisi and Quilled Greatwurm stay in `docs/approximations.md` (Raiding Schemes and Quilled Greatwurm: PLAN-M M10d and M6).

**Purpose:** what remains to be done on card accuracy (approximations, unverified cards) after the consolidation plan (`PLAN-C in docs/history.md`, now condensed in `docs/history.md`), focused on mythics, rares and uncommons (M/R/U), and the options for improving. The lots decided (K0 to K8) and their follow-up are at the end.

## 1. Method

- **Read-only measurements** at commit `ab3dd74`:
  - cards named in a rules test (`packages/engine/test`, Oracle expectations), as `npm run coverage -- --tests` does;
  - matching by name of the entries of `docs/approximations.md` to the cards and their rarity;
  - accepted deviations of `packages/cards/data/audit-baseline.json`;
  - recurring text forms among the untested cards;
  - classification of the 352 per-card entries of `approximations.md` into families (exploration).
- **Limits:**
  - matching by name is heuristic: three false positives were discarded ("The End" in The Endstone, Wan Shi Tong, Carnage);
  - "named in a test" does not prove that the whole text of the card is verified.

### General approximations still in force

- Shock lands put onto the battlefield by an effect: enter tapped without offering to pay 2 life (about 37 cards put a land onto the battlefield); can be lifted with the "as it enters" question of C9.
- "Untap up to N lands": lands chosen automatically (2 cards).
- Mana abilities with a cost ignored by automatic payment (about 18 cards).
- Ability counters, timestamp of a control Aura, convoke and mana abilities.

**Inconsistency:** Cavern of Souls when played is described twice; the entry of the LCI section contradicts the general entry (fixed by C9) and is stale.

## 5. Improvement options

| # | Option | Cost | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Targeted meta fixes:** Rottenmouth Viper, Mosswood Dreadknight, Momo, Deceit | small | immediate on games played |
| 2 | **Low-cost generic levers:** mana in any combination of colors (7 entries, including Vivi and Web Watch), `by: "you"` on `countersPut` (10), `includeSelf` (6), shock lands put onto the battlefield (about 37 cards), script fixes for "timing" (about 15) | small to medium | about 35 entries and one general approximation |
| 3 | **Choices handed back to the player:** kind of counter removed, objects of additional costs on cast, "up to N", untargeted selector (11) | medium | about 45 entries |
| 4 | **Generic duration descriptor** | medium | 19 entries |
| 5 | **Systematic tests** of the 1,518 untested M/R/U: mythics, rares, then uncommons with several abilities, set by set | large (about 12 lots like C13) | about 35 to 40 deviations found |
| 6 | **Automatic checks:** Oracle expectations for the mana of lands (about 70 cards), "enters tapped" (28), Equipment bonuses (14), modes of modal spells (31); semantic audit of targets | medium | about 150 cards verified without a hand-written test |
| 7 | **Official Scryfall rulings:** 1 to 4 per card (sample), turned into tests of delicate interactions | medium | accuracy of edge cases |
| 8 | **Hygiene of `approximations.md`:** label the 129 entries, remove the stale Cavern entry, tidy up | small | readability of the impact |
| 9 | **Player feedback:** "report an error" on a card, reports collected by the server | medium (outside the engine) | deviations seen in real games |

**Recommendation:** 1, 2 and 8 first; then 6, which verifies the most cards per line written; then 5 continuously, mythics and rares first; 3 and 4 depending on the deviations found.
