## 4. Accuracy of cards and approximations

### 4.1 Choices made by the engine in place of the player

These are the deviations that affect the most real games.

**J1. Objects paid as a cost chosen by the engine.** About 100 cards, by family:
- blight as an ability cost (`{T}, Blight 1:`) and blight X (`blightTarget`, `engine/src/stack.ts:1761`, `:2456`);
- collect evidence (22 cards);
- waterbend, convoke and improvise (about 25);
- forage (7);
- exile of cards from the graveyard, removal of counters (10);
- about 19 isolated cards: cycle of the Fears, Gene Pollinator, Radiant Lotus, Leatherhead, Mister Hyde…

In the meta: Deadly Cover-Up (8 copies), Gene Pollinator, Aang, Swift Savior.

Blight as a kicker (Requiting Hex, Pyrrhic Strike) is, for its part, already chosen by the player (`kickerPermanent`, `engine/src/stack.ts:1790`). But its default choice (the cheapest creature, a token first) differs from that of `blightTarget` (a creature that survives first), and the AI does not benefit from it.

**J2. "As it enters, choose…" for a land played or a permanent put onto the battlefield by an effect (read).**
- `playLand` accepts only a basic land type (`landType`, Multiversal Passage; `engine/src/stack.ts:286`). Any other choice goes through `defaultChoice` (`engine/src/replacement.ts:156`), which takes the most present type or color.
- **Cavern of Souls when played** (8 copies, 4 meta decks) therefore names the player's most frequent creature type automatically.
- 13 lands are affected (Three Tree City, Secluded Courtyard, Uncharted Haven, Eclipsed Realms…) and about 40 "as it enters, choose" cards.
- The general entry of `approximations.md` only talks about permanents "put back onto the battlefield by an effect", not played lands. The Cavern test sets `chosen` by hand (`engine/test/lci.test.ts`).

### 5.8 Performance

- **Every `bump` invalidates everything:** the layer cache and the statics index (72 calls, plus 10 direct `s.version += 1`). Some are unconditional: every draw, every life gain.
- **On every rules event,** `liveSources` (`engine/src/triggers.ts`) walks the battlefield, graveyards, stack and command zone again, and takes a snapshot of each source.

- **`consumePlayerEffect`** does no `bump`, whereas `consumeReplacement` does (`engine/src/statics.ts`).
- **Last bench** (10-02, `verify --full`): random 2 players 4,550 decisions/s, 4 players 3,200, high AI 21.7 ms on average (p99 450 ms). Target of 5,000 not reached. The machine (WSL, adjustable CPU power) makes the measurement noisy: before and after must be compared.

## 7. Platform and operations

**Server:**
- **Memory:** importing the rooms and the cards, with no room open, gives 256 MB of RSS (97 MB of heap, measured with `tsx`), for a pm2 limit of 400 MB (`deploy/ecosystem.config.cjs:27`) and 200 rooms allowed. Memory per room is measured nowhere. A restart reloads all the rooms: a restart loop is possible.
- **Production under `tsx`** (a development dependency): `npm ci` also installs Playwright on the VPS.
- **Security, what remains from 09-30:**
  - reconnection tokens in clear in `data/rooms` (files in 644, on a shared machine);
  - no CSP, HSTS only as a comment in nginx;
  - per-address limits bypassable in IPv6 (one address per client in a /64);
  - public `/healthz`.
- **Room resumption:**
  - the `interrupted` set is not persisted: after a second restart, the player reads "This game no longer exists" instead of "interrupted";
  - the `.bad` and `.rules<N>` files are never cleaned up;
  - resumed rooms do not count in the per-address ceiling.
- **No version handshake** between client and server. The service worker and open tabs can run an old client against a new server.
- **Observability:** the console only; no version, no memory, no error counter.

**Local resumption:**
- a save is compared only at checkpoints (every 25 decisions): a divergence in the last 24 goes unnoticed;
- an unknown card (renamed, set removed) makes `defsFor` throw, and the page stays on "Resuming the game…".

**Data:**
- the list of sets is written in three places (`cards/src/sets.ts`, `tools/import-scryfall.ts`, `tools/import-tokens.ts`);
- FDN and FRA are excluded from `import-cards -- all` (touched up by hand);
- legalities are a snapshot of 09-25 to 09-27 and the list of banned cards is written by hand in the README: a rotation or a ban requires three lists to change and a reimport;
- missing French names: 163 in FDN (nos. 565 to 727: matching by collector number), 277 in FRA (no French data; to be checked on Scryfall).

## 11. Verdict

- **The engine remains sound in its foundations.** The rules deviations of 09-30 are fixed, the code is clean, the tests are numerous. The recent sets are better tested than the old ones.
- **Coverage was obtained quickly, and it shows:**
  - the debt has left the monitored lists for surfaces that are not (§ 5.1);
  - `stack.ts` and `legal.ts` keep growing in mirror;
  - approximations have doubled, and 72% of those of the latest sets change the result;
  - the golden games no longer detect anything.
- **For a player, three things matter first:**
  1. a block that can freeze the game (B1);
  2. hidden information visible online (B3);
  3. choices made for them on meta cards: costs in objects (J1), Cavern of Souls when played (J2).
- **Before any new feature,** consolidate in this order: widened safeguards and verification that detects (so that what follows does not regress), real bugs, choices handed back to the player, then reduction of the architecture debt.
